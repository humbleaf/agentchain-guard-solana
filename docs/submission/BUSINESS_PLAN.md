# 💼 Business Plan: AgentChain Guard

## 🎯 The Wedge: Authority as a Service
AI agents are moving from chatbots to economic actors. They need to move money, but humans cannot trust them with private keys. AgentChain Guard provides the "Authority Boundary"—a security primitive that gates settlement behind deterministic receipts.

## 👤 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Primary**: Solana-based AI Agent startups (Framework builders, DePIN operators).
- **Secondary**: DAOs seeking to automate treasury management without losing control.
- **Tertiary**: B2B payments platforms needing granular per-transaction policy enforcement.

## 💎 Value Proposition
- **For Agents**: Access to liquidity within safe, pre-defined boundaries.
- **For Humans**: Full control over agent spending without manual oversight of every transaction.
- **For the Ecosystem**: A standardized safety protocol for the $x402$ Agent Economy.

## 💰 Business Model
1. **SaaS (Policy Gateway)**: Tiered pricing for off-chain policy evaluation and receipt signing.
2. **On-Chain Protocol Fee**: A small bps fee on settlements processed through the Guard program.
3. **Enterprise SDK**: Custom policy modules and private gateway deployments.

## 🚀 Go-To-Market (GTM)
- **Phase 1 (Hackathon)**: Open-source the primitive to gain developer mindshare.
- **Phase 2 (Pilot)**: Integrate with 3 early-stage Solana agent frameworks (e.g., Wayfinder).
- **Phase 3 (Expansion)**: Build a "No-Code Policy Builder" for non-technical treasury managers.

## 🛰️ Why Solana?
Solana is the only chain with the speed and cost-profile to support high-frequency machine payments. Our "Authority Boundary" requires sub-second policy checks and low-cost intent creation—making Solana our natural and only home.

## 🗺️ Roadmap
- **Q3 2026**: ZK-verified policy proofs and Squads/Multisig integration.
- **Q4 2026**: Mainnet launch and "Guard-as-a-Service" API beta.

## ⚠️ Risks
- **Regulation**: Evolution of AI/Crypto compliance.
- **Adoption**: Speed of the Agentic Internet rollout.
- **Competition**: Native wallet policy implementations.
