# 👥 Target Persona Map: AgentChain Guard

## 1. Agent Framework Builders
- **Why care**: Their users are afraid to give agents large budgets.
- **Objection**: "We already have a wallet integration."
- **Angle**: "We don't replace your wallet; we provide a policy-bound vault for your agent actions."
- **Scenario**: "Requires Approval" (High-value payout).

## 2. Solana Wallet Teams (Phantom/Solflare)
- **Why care**: They want to be the portal for the Agentic Internet.
- **Objection**: "This should be a native wallet feature."
- **Angle**: "We're a protocol-level primitive that any wallet can use to surface agent receipts."
- **Scenario**: "Deterministic Receipt" (Transparency).

## 3. Solana Pay / Payment Teams
- **Why care**: High-frequency machine payments are their next growth wedge.
- **Objection**: "Is this fast enough?"
- **Angle**: "LiteSVM-verified settlement with sub-second policy evaluation."
- **Scenario**: "Allowed Payment" (Speed).

## 4. DeFi Protocol Operators
- **Why care**: They want to allow agents to LP/Trade safely.
- **Objection**: "How do we prevent agent drainage?"
- **Angle**: "Per-action budget buckets and allowlisted recipients."
- **Scenario**: "Blocked Payment" (Security).

## 5. Treasury/Multisig Users (Squads)
- **Why care**: They want to automate execution without losing signatory control.
- **Objection**: "We trust our multisig, not your engine."
- **Angle**: "The Guard acts as a pre-authorized boundary; the multisig remains the ultimate authority."
- **Scenario**: "Approval Required" (Signatory Gate).

## 6. Hackathon Judges / Accelerator Reviewers
- **Why care**: They want technical novelty + ecosystem impact.
- **Objection**: "Is this just another multisig?"
- **Angle**: "No, it's a deterministic intent-boundary for machine commerce."
- **Scenario**: "make test-all" (Code Quality).

## 7. AI Infra Founders
- **Why care**: Security is their biggest blocker to adoption.
- **Objection**: "We're focused on LLMs, not payments."
- **Angle**: "We handle the 'touching money' part so you can focus on the 'intelligence' part."
- **Scenario**: "Policy Evaluation" (Modular Rules).
